# Image credits

**Original launch photograph: SpaceX.** It is the first of four photos in SpaceX's post "Liftoff of Starship's first orbital flight" on X, published September 28, 2026: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2104654998034690329 ([archived copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20261003181236/https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2104654998034690329)). It shows the liftoff of Starship Flight 14 from Starbase, Texas, on September 28, 2026. SpaceX also uses this photo, cropped, on its [Starship Flight 14 page](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-14). SpaceX does not name the photographer.

`assets/launch-original.jpg` is the full-size image attached to that post, unchanged (SHA-256 `71f07d0d0ec2d79a86220cc4fad4bcededa5fba3d41c068cb538a82ceab73945`).

The wallpaper editions, the comparison image, and the embedded preview frames are color, exposure, and HDR adaptations of that photograph. The spacecraft, launch site, clouds, and composition have not been redrawn or replaced. No generative imaging was used.
